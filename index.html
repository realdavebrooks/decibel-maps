How this was built

Ticket sales: each state's top-selling concert act on Vivid Seats, 2014 through 2017, as published by Vivid Seats and Stacker, classified by the artist's primary genre (Beyoncé, Bruno Mars and Adele counted as pop). This is the most recent public, all-50-state live-ticket ranking we could find. It is older data and reflects one secondary marketplace.

Search interest: SeatPick's Google Trends study of 18 genres over the 12 months ending Aug. 11, 2026. Each state gets the genre it searches for most relative to other states, not the genre with the most total searches. Delaware's result was not published. Coverage disagrees on Washington, D.C. (Latin in Parade's published list); treat it as unconfirmed. Kentucky's bluegrass counts as a country-family match.

Families: to compare the two views, genres are grouped: country/bluegrass/folk; pop/EDM; rock/metal/punk; soul/R&B/hip-hop/jazz/blues/gospel; indie; Latin; other (classical, reggae).

Sources: Stacker / Vivid Seats · Parade on SeatPick · Hypebot on SeatPick · Mastercard Economics Institute / Live Nation
